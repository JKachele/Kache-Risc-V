0000_0000 - 0FFF_FFFF: DDR3 SDRAM (256MB)
1000_0000 - 6FFF_FFFF: Reserved
7000_0000 - 7007_FFFF: BRAM (512KB)
        Reset-Vector: 7000-0000
8000_0000 - FFFF_FFFF: IO
        8000_0000 - 80FF_FFFF: QSPI Flash (16MB)
        9000_0000 - 9000_00FF: UART
                9000_0000: UART Data
                9000_0004: UART Control
        F000_0000 - FFFF_FFFF: Basic I/O
                F000_0000: Arty White LEDs
